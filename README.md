One image summing up 19 November 2026 world news: Trump, Xi and Putin meeting in Shenzhen, the Crimean Bridge attacked again, Anthropic's record $81.3B IPO, and an Israeli ground operation in Gaza.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies the copy is a valid 1024x1024 PNG.

## How the image was made

The entry is a single 1024x1024 PNG. A header names the day — 19 NOVEMBER 2026 — above a 2x2 grid of the day's four most consequential stories, each with a bold headline and a short summary:

1. **Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — Trump arrived in China for the APEC summit; Xi hugged him on the tarmac. The three leaders are set for a trilateral meeting on Thursday.
2. **Crimean Bridge attacked again** — A Russian official said the strategic bridge linking Crimea to Russia was hit again; traffic is suspended after Ukrainian strikes damaged the key supply route.
3. **Anthropic's record $81.3B IPO** — The maker of Claude priced shares at $83, raising a record $81.3B at a ~$1.5T valuation. Shares start trading on Nasdaq as ANT on Thursday — the first frontier AI lab to go public.
4. **Israeli ground operation in Gaza** — Israel's military launched a rare ground operation in central Gaza beyond the ceasefire line; tanks advanced as dozens of displaced Palestinians fled their shelters.
